Smart Run Beats & Smart Workout Music

Original workout audio for the Smart Run Beats and Smart Workout Music Connect IQ apps (Garmin music watches). The apps download these files to your watch over Wi-Fi.

All audio is generated from scratch by code (no samples, no third-party music) and is released under CC0 1.0 (public domain).
